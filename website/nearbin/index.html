How to Install NearBin on Android

Since NearBin is distributed directly for rapid civic deployment, follow these 3 quick steps:

1

Download APK

Tap the Download NearBin.apk button. If your browser displays "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Tap the downloaded file from notifications. If Android prompts for permission, tap Settings and turn on "Allow from this source".

3

Install & Report

Tap Install. Once installed, open NearBin, sign in via Google or Mobile OTP, and start monitoring clean streets!

Engineered for Indian Streets

A high-precision, low-battery waste tracking ecosystem built for citizens, municipal workers, and recyclers.

🗺️

Snapchat-Style Garbage Heatmap

Watch garbage density hotspots glow across street-level Mappls & Carto maps. Tapping any hotspot reveals the latest community photos and stories.

📸

Live Camera GPS Watermark

Zero-friction camera capture with auto-stamped GPS coordinates, preventing fake gallery submissions and ensuring pinpoint municipal accountability.

🎯

Instant GPS Recenter

One-tap accuracy lock zooms straight into your physical street position anywhere in India, no matter how far you've panned.

🧹

Govt Safai Mitra Portal

Municipal workers access an active queue of reported dumps, clean them up, and upload mandatory "After Photos" to mark them resolved.

♻️

Kabadiwala Scrap Radar

Local scrap collectors can filter high-value plastic and metal waste, claiming materials before they reach landfills.

🌓

OLED Dark & Daylight Modes

Ultra battery-saving pure black OLED dark mode for night reporting, plus a crisp daylight theme for bright outdoor sun.
