2. Purpose & Civic Mission of NearBin

NearBin is an open civic technology and environmental intelligence tool developed to support clean neighborhood initiatives, the Swachh Bharat mission, and circular zero-waste economies.

NearBin enables citizens to:

  • Photograph and geo-tag public garbage dumps, overflow bins, and litter hotspots in real time.
  • View live community heatmaps of public sanitation conditions.
  • Coordinate with municipal sanitation workers (Safai Mitras) and scrap recyclers (Kabadiwalas) to facilitate rapid cleanup and recycling of paper, plastic, and metal waste.
  • Earn citizen Swachhata Karma points, civic leaderboard recognition, and community badges.

4. Civic Reporting Standards & Prohibited Conduct

The integrity of NearBin relies upon honest community participation. As a condition of your use of NearBin, you warrant and agree that you will NOT:

  • Submit false, staged, fabricated, or duplicate reports of garbage or litter.
  • Upload images that depict private individuals without their consent, lewd or obscene material, harassment, hate speech, or content unrelated to civic waste.
  • Mark clean sites as contaminated, or falsely certify contaminated sites as cleaned.
  • Trespass onto private property, restricted government compounds, or hazardous zones to photograph garbage.
  • Engage in automated scraping, reverse engineering, rate-limit bypassing, or denial-of-service attacks against NearBin APIs or database servers.
  • Impersonate any government municipal officer, sanitation supervisor, Safai Mitra, or another citizen.
Zero-Tolerance Abuse Policy:
Accounts found submitting fraudulent reports, abusing the Karma points mechanism, or uploading malicious media will be permanently banned and submitted entries removed from the public civic map.

7. Personal Safety & Hazardous Waste Notice

Safety First:
Citizens should only photograph garbage dumps from safe public sidewalks, roadsides, or designated community spots. Never touch or handle unidentified chemicals, medical/biohazard waste, live electric wires, or sharp industrial scraps. Always prioritize personal safety over reporting.
